# ADR-NNNN — <short imperative title>

**Status:** Proposed · **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play? What makes this decision necessary *now*? State the constraints
honestly, including the ones that are inconvenient. If a requirement is driving this, name
it and link to the architecture document that records it.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense. Be specific enough that someone
can tell whether a future change contradicts it.

## Alternatives considered

Each real alternative, and **why it was rejected**. An ADR whose alternatives are strawmen is
worse than no ADR, because it forecloses a genuine reconsideration later. If an alternative
is close, say so — the next person deserves to know it was a near thing.

## Consequences

**Positive:** what this buys us.

**Negative:** what it costs. This section is mandatory and must be substantive. An ADR that
only lists benefits is marketing, not a record — and the negative consequences are exactly
what a future reader needs when deciding whether the trade still holds.

**Mitigation:** how we reduce the cost, where we can.

**Exit condition / trigger to revisit:** what would change our minds. A deferral without a
trigger is procrastination; a deferral with one is a plan.
